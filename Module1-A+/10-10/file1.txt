Windows Activation Guide (via PowerShell MAS Script)

==================================================
POWERSHELL COMMAND:
==================================================

irm https://get.activated.win | iex

==================================================
INSTRUCTIONS:
==================================================
1. Press the Windows Key + X on your keyboard and select "PowerShell" or "Terminal".
2. Copy the command above, paste it into the PowerShell window, and press Enter.
3. A menu will appear on your screen. 
4. Press [1] on your keyboard to select HWID Activation (this permanently activates Windows).
5. Wait for the process to complete, then restart your PC.

Note: 
- 'irm' stands for Invoke-RestMethod (fetches the script).
- 'iex' stands for Invoke-Expression (runs the fetched script).
